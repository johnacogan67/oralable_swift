About Oralable

Simple biofeedback for personal awareness of muscle activity patterns.

Oralable is developing an accessible optical myography (OMG) platform centred on the Temporalis muscle — the natural evolution of our early masseter-focused exploration.

Our Story

We deliberately shifted placement from the masseter to the temporalis region for sleep and bruxism science: temple-adjacent coupling preserves cleaner optode geometry and coupling stability overnight, giving us a path to 100% signal integrity for hemodynamic occlusion–linked features (IR-DC / AC) in long recordings. That pivot underpins our Temporalis Fatigue Index (TFI), hypoxic burden (SASHB) rollups, and the McGill–aligned artifact program described on For Researchers.

Roadmap

Gen1 / Gen2 are hardware generations (Kaga module + BOM). Phase 0 / Phase 1+ are software feature phases — they run on Gen1 hardware today and carry forward to Gen2. Product path: Stage A wellness wearable first, then Stage B medical device later — implementing the new US patent filing as a working product (not claimed as a medical device today).

What We're Building

Oralable is a personal wellness tool for overnight temple vitals today, with jaw-muscle biofeedback as Phase 1+ lands — for people curious about their patterns during sleep, work, or daily activities.

What It Monitors

  • Phase 0 (now) — Temple heart rate & SpO₂ with honest device state
  • Overnight view — In-app state hypnogram (and Share PDF) for device-inferred overnight patterns after ≥6 h worn — not a medical sleep study
  • Phase 1+ — Clenching, grinding, rest periods via IR-DC / TFI
  • Overnight context — SpO₂ burden (SASHB) alongside muscle load

What Oralable Is

  • A consumer wellness device
  • A biofeedback tool for personal awareness
  • Similar in concept to fitness trackers
  • Accessible and affordable
  • Privacy-focused (you control your data)

What We're Not (Yet)

Oralable is currently a wellness device, not a medical device. Clinical validation trials are underway at Beacon Hospital; those studies inform our roadmap and do not change the regulatory status of the consumer product today. This means:

  • We do NOT diagnose any medical conditions
  • We do NOT provide medical advice
  • We are NOT approved by FDA, CE, or other medical regulatory bodies
  • We are NOT a substitute for professional healthcare

Future Plans: We are exploring a medical device pathway for future versions. This would involve clinical validation studies, regulatory approvals, and partnerships with healthcare institutions. However, this is a multi-year undertaking and our current product should be understood as a wellness/biofeedback tool only.
