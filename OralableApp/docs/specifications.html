Oralable Gen 2 — PCB00003 · PCB REV11 · BOM REV9 · Kaga ES4L15BA1 · firmware in bring-up · kits not yet shipped

Hardware identity

Hardware generation Gen 2 (bring-up · kits not shipped)
BOM PCB00003-TGM-BOM-REV9
PCB production / assembly REV11
Board target (firmware) pcb00003_gen2 · nRF54L15 (port of the Gen 1 app)
BLE module (U5) Kaga ES4L15BA1
Battery LP270829 35 mAh
Firmware (pilot) 2.0.x target (not flashed). Installed the same way as Gen 1, over SWD then RTT.
Software phase (now) Vitals Phase 0 (temple HR & SpO₂)
Overnight UI (patient app) State hypnogram (Dashboard + Share) · Clinical Temporalis PDF · bands unlock after ≥1 h worn; aim ≥6 h for a full night view

Sensors

PPG Sensor MAXM86161 (Analog Devices)
PPG Wavelengths Infrared (880nm), Red (660nm), Green (520nm)
Accelerometer LIS2DTW12 (STMicroelectronics)
Accelerometer Range ±2g, 14-bit resolution
Temperature On-chip sensor

Sampling Rates

PPG (all channels) 50 Hz
Accelerometer 100 Hz
Temperature 1 Hz
Battery 0.1 Hz

Wireless

Protocol Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0
Processor nRF54L15 (Nordic Semiconductor)
BLE module (U5) Kaga ES4L15BA1 (nRF54L15)
Range ~10 meters (typical indoor)
Firmware 2.0.x target (not flashed). Kits not shipped.

Power

Battery LiPo rechargeable (LP270829, 35 mAh)
Runtime 6-8 hours continuous (typical)
Charging Oralable charging case (USB-C). Seat the clip so the coils align. Not Qi / MagSafe / a phone wireless pad
Status LED Green only. On the case, no phone: flashing green while charging; a short green pulse when hold is reached. Off the case, not connected: dark. Never red or blue
Charge Time ~2 hours (typical)

Physical

Dimensions 26.2 × 9.1 × 8.2 mm
Weight ~2 grams
Housing Formlabs Dental LT Clear V2 resin
Water Resistance Splash resistant (not submersible)

Placement & Vitals Phase

Primary site (pilot) Temple / temporalis (Vitals Phase 0 — HR & SpO₂)
Worn / dock sensing Automatic worn = IR pulse (not die temperature). Status LEDs are green-only
Muscle-event algorithms Phase 1+ after vitals gates (clench / IR-DC occlusion research)

Compatibility

iOS iPhone 12 and later, iOS 16+
Android Not currently supported
App Oralable 4.3.3 (build 5) on the App Store / TestFlight
OTA updates MCUboot / mcumgr via Nordic Device Manager (BLE)

Gen 1 is frozen (REV10, ES2832AA2). It is not the device partners or patients are shown. Firmware for Gen 2 is a port of that app and is not on the boards yet.
