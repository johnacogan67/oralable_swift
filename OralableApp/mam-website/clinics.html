The offer

This is the rate card. It is not a bill for nights already run. The company keeps the hardware. The clinic does not buy the kit.

Each leased kit includes:

  • One temple wearable. It is the unit now in test. No kit has shipped.

  • The iOS TestFlight app, version 4.3.3.
